## Projects in progress

### learn2earndao

Learning-site initiative that connects **learning**, **earning**, and **community**. The goal is to give learners a clear path from skills to real work, and to give employers and clients a place to find help—whether for security, web admin, customer service, continuous improvement, or custom consulting. The site will host services, products, and consulting that I (and eventually others) offer, all supported by a learning platform and customized tools.

**Platform and treasury:** Work has started building on top of the **Docker Frappe ERP** platform, tied directly to the learn2earndao site and its **financial treasury management**. The stack will include **decentralized architecture** and treat **Web3** as first-class citizens—with native components and integrations for the platform.

### Customized AI models

Specialized models to support the learn2earndao site and related work:

- **Security administration** — Assist with access, policies, and safe operations.
- **Web admin** — Help manage site infrastructure, content, and updates.
- **Basic customer service** — Triage, FAQs, and lightweight support.
- **Continuous improvement** — Suggest and track improvements to the site, services, and products.

These models are designed to extend as new services, products, and consulting offerings are added.

### Learning platform + spreadsheet labs

Part of the same ecosystem as learn2earndao and standards work. Hands-on labs and a learning community built around:

- **Excel 365** — LAMBDA/dynamic arrays, formula architecture, testable workbooks.
- **VBA** — Automation, tooling, teaching patterns.
- **TypeScript** — Add-ins and modern spreadsheet workflows.
- **Standards** — ODF/ODS + OOXML, schema-first thinking, conversion and validation.

See the main repo README for tracks, layout, and goals.
